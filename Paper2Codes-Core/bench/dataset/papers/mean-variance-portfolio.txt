The Global Minimum-Variance Portfolio

Abstract. We derive the closed-form weights of the global minimum-variance
(GMV) portfolio, the fully-invested portfolio with the smallest possible
variance given a covariance matrix of asset returns.

1. Problem. Given the covariance matrix Sigma of N assets, choose portfolio
weights w (summing to one) that minimise the portfolio variance wᵀ Sigma w.

2. Solution (Algorithm 1). With 1 the vector of ones, the GMV weights are

  Equation (1): w = (Sigma^{-1} 1) / (1ᵀ Sigma^{-1} 1)

3. Implementation notes. The implementation should accept a covariance matrix,
return weights that sum to one, and expose the resulting portfolio variance.
Validate that the weights sum to 1 and that the variance is no larger than that
of any equal-weight portfolio on a small example.
