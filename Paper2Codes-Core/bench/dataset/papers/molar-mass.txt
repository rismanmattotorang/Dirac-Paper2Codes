Molar Mass from a Chemical Formula

Abstract. Compute the molar mass of a compound from its chemical formula by
summing atomic weights.

1. Method (Algorithm 1). Parse the formula into element-count pairs (e.g. H2O ->
{H:2, O:1}) and sum count * atomic_weight over elements.

2. Implementation notes. Support multi-letter elements (e.g. Na, Cl) and counts.
Validate that H2O is approximately 18.015 g/mol.
