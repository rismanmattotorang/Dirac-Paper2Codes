Measurement Probabilities of a Qubit State

Abstract. Measuring a qubit in the computational basis yields outcome k with
probability |amplitude_k|^2 (the Born rule).

1. Definition. For state vector psi:
  Equation (1): P(k) = |psi_k|^2,  with sum_k P(k) = 1

2. Implementation notes. Given complex amplitudes, return the measurement
probabilities. Validate that they are non-negative and sum to 1; reject
unnormalised states beyond a tolerance.
