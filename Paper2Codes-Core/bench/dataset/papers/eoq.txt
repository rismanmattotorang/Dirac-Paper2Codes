The Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) Inventory Model

Abstract. The Economic Order Quantity model determines the order size that
minimises the total annual inventory cost, balancing ordering cost against
holding cost.

1. Model. With annual demand D, fixed ordering cost per order S, and annual
holding cost per unit H, the total cost as a function of order quantity Q is

  Equation (1): TC(Q) = (D / Q) * S + (Q / 2) * H

2. Optimum (Algorithm 1). Minimising TC over Q yields the closed-form optimal
order quantity

  Equation (2): Q* = sqrt(2 * D * S / H)

3. Implementation notes. The implementation should compute Q* and the
corresponding total cost, validating D, S, H > 0. Verify that TC(Q*) equals
sqrt(2 * D * S * H) and that ordering cost equals holding cost at the optimum.
