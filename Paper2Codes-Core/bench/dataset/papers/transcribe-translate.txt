DNA Transcription and Translation

Abstract. The central dogma maps DNA to RNA (transcription) and RNA to a protein
sequence (translation) via the genetic code.

1. Method (Algorithm 1). Transcription replaces T with U. Translation reads RNA
in codons (length-3) using the standard codon table until a stop codon.

2. Implementation notes. Provide transcribe(dna) -> rna and translate(rna) ->
protein (single-letter amino acids), stopping at the first stop codon. Validate
that "ATG...TAA" begins with M and terminates at the stop.
