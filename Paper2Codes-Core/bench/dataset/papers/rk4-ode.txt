Fourth-Order Runge-Kutta Integration of ODEs

Abstract. RK4 is a classic explicit one-step method for solving dy/dt = f(t, y).

1. Method (Algorithm 1). Given step h:
  Equation (1): k1 = f(t, y)
  Equation (2): k2 = f(t + h/2, y + h k1/2)
  Equation (3): k3 = f(t + h/2, y + h k2/2)
  Equation (4): k4 = f(t + h, y + h k3)
  Equation (5): y_{n+1} = y_n + (h/6)(k1 + 2 k2 + 2 k3 + k4)

2. Implementation notes. Integrate a given f from t0 to t1. Validate on
dy/dt = y (exponential) against the analytic solution within tolerance.
