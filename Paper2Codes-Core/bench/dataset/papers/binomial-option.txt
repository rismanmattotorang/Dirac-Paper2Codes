Cox-Ross-Rubinstein Binomial Option Pricing

Abstract. The CRR binomial model prices a European option by discretising the
underlying's evolution into an N-step recombining tree.

1. Method (Algorithm 1). With spot S, strike K, rate r, volatility sigma, and
maturity T over N steps (dt = T/N):
  Equation (1): u = exp(sigma sqrt(dt)),  d = 1/u
  Equation (2): p = (exp(r dt) - d) / (u - d)
Terminal payoffs are max(S u^j d^{N-j} - K, 0); discount back by p, (1-p), e^{-r dt}.

2. Implementation notes. Return the European call price; as N grows it should
converge to the Black-Scholes value.
