Projectile Motion under Uniform Gravity

Abstract. Closed-form range and flight time of a projectile launched on flat
ground, neglecting air resistance.

1. Model. For launch speed v0, angle theta, gravity g:
  Equation (1): time_of_flight = 2 v0 sin(theta) / g
  Equation (2): range = v0^2 sin(2 theta) / g
  Equation (3): max_height = (v0 sin(theta))^2 / (2 g)

2. Implementation notes. Return range, flight time, and max height. Validate
that maximum range occurs at theta = 45 degrees.
