The Lennard-Jones Potential for Pairwise Molecular Interactions

Abstract. We describe the Lennard-Jones (12-6) potential, a standard model for
the interaction energy between a pair of neutral atoms or molecules as a function
of their separation.

1. Model. Given the separation distance r, the depth of the potential well
epsilon, and the distance sigma at which the potential is zero, the interaction
energy is

  Equation (1): V(r) = 4 * epsilon * ((sigma / r)^12 - (sigma / r)^6)

2. Properties. The potential has a minimum at r_min = 2^(1/6) * sigma, where
V(r_min) = -epsilon. The r^-12 term models short-range Pauli repulsion and the
r^-6 term models long-range van der Waals attraction.

3. Implementation notes. The implementation should compute V(r) for given
epsilon, sigma, and r, and expose the well minimum. Energies are reported in the
same units as epsilon. Verify that the minimum occurs at 2^(1/6)*sigma and equals
-epsilon.
