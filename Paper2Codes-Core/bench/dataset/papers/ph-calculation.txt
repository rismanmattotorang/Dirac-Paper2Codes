pH from Hydrogen Ion Concentration

Abstract. The pH of an aqueous solution is the negative base-10 logarithm of the
hydrogen-ion activity.

1. Definition.
  Equation (1): pH = -log10([H+])
  Equation (2): pOH = 14 - pH    (at 25 C)

2. Implementation notes. Given [H+] in mol/L (> 0), return pH (and pOH).
Validate that [H+] = 1e-7 yields pH 7 (neutral).
