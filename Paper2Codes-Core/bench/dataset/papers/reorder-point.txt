Inventory Reorder Point with Safety Stock

Abstract. The reorder point (ROP) triggers replenishment to cover demand during
the supplier lead time, plus a safety buffer against variability.

1. Model. For mean daily demand d, lead time L (days), demand standard deviation
sigma_d, and service-level z-score z:
  Equation (1): safety_stock = z * sigma_d * sqrt(L)
  Equation (2): ROP = d * L + safety_stock

2. Implementation notes. Return the reorder point and safety stock. Validate
that zero variability (sigma_d = 0) yields ROP = d * L.
