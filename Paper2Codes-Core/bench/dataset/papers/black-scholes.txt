Closed-Form Pricing of European Options under the Black-Scholes Model

Abstract. We present the Black-Scholes formula for pricing European call and put
options on a non-dividend-paying asset. Given the current spot price S, strike K,
risk-free rate r, volatility sigma, and time to maturity T, the price of a
European call is given in closed form.

1. Model. The asset price follows geometric Brownian motion. Under the
risk-neutral measure, the discounted price is a martingale.

2. Method (Algorithm 1). Compute the auxiliary quantities

  Equation (1): d1 = (ln(S/K) + (r + sigma^2 / 2) * T) / (sigma * sqrt(T))
  Equation (2): d2 = d1 - sigma * sqrt(T)

The European call price is

  Equation (3): C = S * N(d1) - K * exp(-r * T) * N(d2)

and by put-call parity the put price is

  Equation (4): P = K * exp(-r * T) * N(-d2) - S * N(-d1)

where N(.) is the standard normal cumulative distribution function.

3. Implementation notes. The implementation should accept (S, K, r, sigma, T)
and return the call and put prices. Validate against put-call parity:
C - P = S - K * exp(-r * T).
